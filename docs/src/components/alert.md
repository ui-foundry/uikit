# Alert <span class="text-size-3.5">`<ui-alert/>`</span>

The **Alert** component is used to convey important information to the user. It can be used to display error messages, warnings, or other critical notifications that require the user's attention.

<ui-alert heading="This is a primary alert">
	<p>Please verify your billing information and try again.</p>
          <ul className="list-inside list-disc text-sm">
            <li>Check your card details</li>
            <li>Ensure sufficient funds</li>
            <li>Verify billing address</li>
          </ul>
</ui-alert>
